What uncertainty looks like here

There are two complementary views: variation across bootstrap samples and the shape of class-probability distributions.

  • Wide bootstrap spread suggests evaluation instability.
  • Diffuse class probabilities suggest ambiguity for the evaluated image set.
  • A sharp probability peak is not proof of correctness under distribution shift.
